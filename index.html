What gets automated

A focused workflow for teams that receive leads through forms, email, booking tools, or CRM entries and need faster response without more admin work.

1. Capture

New lead arrives from a form, inbox, calendar booking, or CRM source.

2. Clean

Key fields are normalized so names, channels, notes, and urgency stay usable.

3. Classify

AI tags intent, priority, service fit, and missing information.

4. Draft

A reply draft or follow-up note is generated for review.

5. Log

The lead is written into a sheet or CRM and routed to the right person.
